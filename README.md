Content Research – THREAD
1. Organisation Background

THREAD is a proposed contemporary fashion and streetwear retail store aimed at teenagers and young adults. The brand focuses on fashion as a form of personal expression, offering clothing and accessories influenced by modern trends, streetwear and different fashion aesthetics.

The website will combine an online retail experience with fashion inspiration. This will allow visitors to browse products while also discovering outfit ideas, collections and fashion-related content.

2. Target Audience Research

The main target audience for THREAD is teenagers, young adults, fashion-conscious consumers and streetwear enthusiasts. This audience is generally interested in current fashion trends, personal style and visually appealing content.

Because the target audience is likely to interact with fashion content online, the website will use strong photography, simple navigation, product displays and editorial-style content to make the website engaging and easy to use.

3. Product Content

The THREAD website will feature a range of contemporary clothing and accessories. Proposed products include:

T-shirts
Hoodies
Jeans
Cargo pants
Jackets
Tops
Dresses
Caps
Bags
Accessories

Products will be displayed with images, names, prices and short descriptions. Products will also be organised into collections to make browsing easier.

4. Collection Research

The website will organise products into different collections to help users find styles that interest them. Proposed collections include:

New Arrivals – Recently added products.
Streetwear – Casual and urban-inspired clothing.
Y2K – Clothing influenced by late-1990s and early-2000s fashion.
Essentials – Simple and versatile everyday clothing.
Seasonal Collections – Products based on current or upcoming seasons.

This structure will make the Shop section easier to navigate and will allow products to be filtered according to different categories.

5. Lookbook Content

The Lookbook will provide visual outfit inspiration. Instead of only displaying individual products, it will show how different THREAD items can be combined into complete outfits.

The Lookbook will contain high-quality fashion images accompanied by short descriptions or information about the featured clothing. This section is intended to help customers visualise how products can be worn.

6. Style Journal

The Style Journal will provide fashion-related content and help make the website more than just an online store.

Possible topics include:

Current fashion trends
Outfit ideas
Styling guides
Seasonal fashion
Streetwear inspiration
Fashion aesthetics
How to style specific clothing items

The journal will use images alongside short, readable articles to maintain the website's visual and editorial style.

7. Visual Content Research

Visual content will be an important part of THREAD's website because fashion relies heavily on photography and visual presentation. Product photography will be used for the Shop and Collections pages, while editorial-style images will be used for the Lookbook and Style Journal.

Images used in the website will either be created specifically for the project or sourced from websites that allow appropriate use. Copyright and licensing requirements will be considered when selecting external images.

8. Brand Content

THREAD's brand identity will focus on individuality, modern fashion and youth culture. The website's content will therefore use a confident but approachable tone.

The proposed tagline is:

“Style, culture and everything in between.”

The content will avoid overly formal language and instead use short, engaging descriptions that suit the intended audience.

Home

│   ├── Streetwear
│   ├── Y2K
│   └── Essentials

This structure allows users to move easily between shopping, fashion inspiration and information about the brand.

10. Content Requirements

The website will require the following content:

Brand logo
Product names
Product descriptions
Product prices
Product photographs
Collection descriptions
Lookbook photographs
Style Journal articles
About-page content
Contact information
Navigation labels
Call-to-action buttons

All content will be reviewed before implementation to ensure that it is relevant to THREAD's target audience and consistent with the overall brand identity.

11. Research Influence on the Website

The content research influenced the website by establishing a clear focus on fashion retail, visual storytelling and easy product discovery. The combination of a Shop section, product collections, Lookbook and Style Journal allows THREAD to function as both a retail website and a source of fashion inspiration.

The research also supports the use of a visually focused design, clear product categorisation and concise content to create an engaging experience for the target audience.
